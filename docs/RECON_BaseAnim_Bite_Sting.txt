*** THE MAXRELOAD 23 CARRIES NO REBUILD-vs-MIXED SIGNAL AT ALL. ***
*** IT IS 23/23 AN ARRAY BASE ADDRESS.  THE BRIEF PREDICTED THIS. ***
=====================================================================
The brief asked me to partition the reloads before reading anything into the
count of 23.  Partitioned, the answer is unambiguous:

  `.Ledf04` x23  -> DATA-LABEL ADDRESS, not a constant.
  `.Ledf04` is `.incrom 0xedf04, 0xedf58` -- an 84-BYTE TABLE -- and every one
  of the 23 reloads is followed within two instructions by
      ldrb rN, [rBase, rIndex]
  i.e. it is `tbl[i]` on a file-scope `const u8` array, subscripted with a
  VARIABLE index at 23 separate sites.  gcc rebuilds the base at each one
  because the reg+reg byte-load addressing mode needs it in a register.
  THERE IS NOTHING TO PIN AND NOTHING TO REUSE: write the array as a plain
  `extern unsigned char Ledf04[] __asm__(".Ledf04");` and subscript it, and the
  23 reloads come out on their own.

Full partition of 89 pool refs, 42 distinct:
    SYMBOL addr 37   DATA-LABEL addr 34   NUMERIC const 15
    ABS addr-ish 2   IO-REG addr 1
Highest NUMERIC reload: 0x7828 x5.  `mov rlo,rhigh` copies: 35 (the FEWEST of
brief G's four), plus 31 `mov rlo,rlo`.
Only 15 of 89 pool refs are true numeric constants -- the LOWEST ratio of the
four.  So on the partitioned screen this is the CLOSEST of the four to a pure
rebuild, which is the exact opposite of what "maxreload 23, the highest in the
batch" suggests on its face.  Still MIXED (5 reloads, 35 reuse copies), so no
blanket pin pass -- but if any of the four ever rewards a pin pass it is this
one, and the candidates are the 15 numeric constants, NOT the 23.

CONCLUSION, stated for the record because the brief asked: THE RELOAD COUNT
MUST BE PARTITIONED BEFORE IT IS READ, and once partitioned it predicted the
OPPOSITE ranking.  An unpartitioned maxreload is not a weak signal, it is an
actively misleading one.
