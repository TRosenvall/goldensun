0.  THE FRAME, MEASURED ON SIX GREPS, AND THE AGGREGATE COLUMN RESOLVED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

The four-grep recipe is not enough.  Two further address-forming idioms exist
and both appear in this group:

  FIFTH  `add rX, sp`         (bare, no immediate)
  SIXTH  `mov rX, #K` then `add rX, sp`   -- immediate FIRST, sp added into it

The sixth is how you get a stack address into a HIGH register, which
`add rX, sp, #K` cannot do (it needs a low rd).  BaseAnim_Tiamat uses it twice.
Neither is covered by the four-grep recipe or by docs/elevation.md's
`mov rX, sp / add rX, #K` note, which is the fifth form in the other operand
order.

  function                frame  sub sp  reg-built  mov rX,sp  add rX,sp,#K  bare add rX,sp  str[sp] no load
  Anim_ScreenShatter      0x38   yes     no         0          0             0               7
  BaseAnim_Tiamat         0x48   yes     no         0          1 (0x3c)      2 (both 0x3c)   9
  BaseAnim_ParticleSpray  0x9c   yes     no         0          3 (0x88,0x8c,0x90) 0          32
  Anim_Neptune            0x74   yes     no         1 (0x50)   4 (0x28,0x48,0x58,0x64) 0     19

AGGREGATES, every site resolved by READING ITS REGION, block-aware:

  Anim_ScreenShatter      0  EXACT -- zero address-forming forms of ANY kind,
                             so no base register is ever materialised and no
                             store can hide behind one.  All 71 `[sp]` accesses
                             are word ldr/str.
  BaseAnim_Tiamat         1  EXACT -- sp+0x3c's address is passed to
                             GetBattleActorPos2 and used at [r6] and [r6,#4].
                             The two sixth-form sites are the SAME offset, so
                             they are extra references to one object.
  BaseAnim_ParticleSpray  3  EXACT -- 0x8c and 0x88 are stored to [sp]/[sp,#4]
                             as outgoing args 5 and 6 of Anim_Djinni;
                             0x90 goes to Func_80e3944 as a vec3_t out-param.
  Anim_Neptune            4  *** THE BRIEF SAYS 5.  ONE SITE IS NOT AN
                             AGGREGATE. ***  sp+0x28 is
                             `add r4,sp,#0x28 / ldrh r4,[r4] / strh r4,[r3,#4]`
                             -- ONE sub-word load with the base overwritten by
                             its own result on the next instruction.  It is a
                             u16 SCALAR.  Thumb-1 has no sp-relative ldrh, so a
                             sub-word stack scalar MUST materialise a base and
                             is indistinguishable from an aggregate at the grep.

  AND THE TRAP, WHICH COST NOTHING ONLY BECAUSE THE SCAN WAS BLOCK-AWARE:
  Neptune's sp+0x48 is a genuine aggregate (`mov r2,r7` -- passed as an
  argument), but THREE BLOCKS LATER r7 IS REASSIGNED, `ldr r7, =gBuffer`, and
  then walked: [r7], [r7,#4], [r7,#0xc], [r7,#0x10], [r7,#0x18], `add r7,#0x1c`.
  A first-use scan that does not stop at a label reads those six offsets as the
  STACK object and reports a 0x1c-byte aggregate at sp+0x48.  It is a particle
  walk over gBuffer and has nothing to do with sp.  STOP AT EVERY LABEL AND
  BRANCH, and re-check whether the base register is still the one you tracked.

  A SEVENTH CASE for the recipe: an address-taken SCALAR (ParticleSpray's
  0x88/0x8c -- `int a; f(&a);`) also surfaces at this grep and is neither an
  aggregate nor a sub-word scalar.  The question the grep actually answers is
  "is a base register materialised at all"; when one is, only reading the
  region settles what it is.

  NET EFFECT ON THE BRIEF'S SELECTION PREMISE: the column reads 0/1/3/4 instead
  of 0/1/3/5.  The ORDERING is unchanged, so the premise survives and
  ScreenShatter leading is confirmed on the strongest form of the test.

1.  THE COMPARISON CENSUS -- AND IT MUST BE RE-RUN AS A LOOP CENSUS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Raw mnemonic counts on the reference bodies:

  function                bne  signed(blt/ble/bgt/bge)  beq  bhi  bls  insns  pool
  Anim_ScreenShatter       16   48                        2    1    0   1044    15
  BaseAnim_Tiamat          41   14                       12    5    1    977     2
  BaseAnim_ParticleSpray   68   15                       27    1    2   1595     1
  Anim_Neptune             35   55                        8    5    1   1466    13

THE RAW CENSUS MIS-SIGNALS, AND ANIM_SCREENSHATTER IS THE PROOF.  Its 48-to-16
signed majority is the brief's headline reason not to import Anim_Gaia's all-`!=`
rule.  I classified every backward edge in it: ELEVEN loops close the function
and TEN close on `bne`/`beq`.  Exactly one, the midpoint-circle inner loop, is a
signed compare (`while (i >= j)`).  All 48 signed compares but one are
coordinate CLAMPS, signed-division bias corrections and frame-phase tests -- not
loops at all.  Gaia's rule TRANSFERS almost completely and is in the delivered
body.

So before using these four rows: count only branches that CLOSE A BACKWARD EDGE.
RECON_b311b_four states this as a caveat about `bhi`/`bls` and notices it locally
for ScreenShatter's tilemap pair; it generalises, and on a clamp-heavy or
division-heavy function it inverts the headline.  Tiamat and ParticleSpray are
bne-dominant on the raw count too, so for those two the raw and loop censuses
are likely to agree -- but that is a prediction, not a measurement, and Neptune
(55 signed) is the one where the re-count matters most and has NOT been done.

Build these with a regex that accepts BOTH `#0x28` and `#8`: small immediates
are printed WITHOUT the 0x prefix and a `#0x`-only regex silently drops every
slot below 0x10.  Mine did, and it manufactured a phantom hole I then chased.
(Independently flagged by the coordinator from a sibling agent's work -- two
separate discoveries of the same instrument defect in one batch.)

4.  WHAT TRANSFERS FROM THE DELIVERED SCREENSHATTER PARK
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Full detail and figures are in PARK_Anim_ScreenShatter.c's header.  The items
most likely to matter on these three:

(a) A TILED-SURFACE PLOT INDEX MUST BE A FLAT SUM WITH `<<`, NOT `*`
    (437 -> 511 of 1132, the batch's biggest single move).  When the scale
    factors are all multiples of a common power of two -- 8, 0x40, 0x800 for an
    8x8 tiled surface -- fold FACTORS THE POWER OUT and emits a Horner form with
    extra `lsl` per site.  Shifts leave it nothing to factor.  Any function in
    this bank that writes a tiled buffer by coordinate is exposed.

(b) BUT THE DIVISIONS STAY DIVISIONS.  `x / 8` is `cmp x,#0 / bge / add x,#7 /
    asr x,#3`; the bias correction only exists for signed division, and `>> 3`
    is a bare `asr`.  `/ 2` takes gcc's other shape (`lsr #31 / add / asr #1`).
    Mixing shifts for the multiplies with `/` for the divisions in one
    expression is the lever, and it is not obvious.

(c) DO NOT READ AN ACCUMULATION ORDER BACK OUT AS PARENTHESISATION -- measured
    FIFTY encodings NEGATIVE (511 -> 461).  fold reassociates a flat sum and
    cse1 then rebuilds the reference's grouping from the shared subexpressions;
    writing the grouping by hand pins it and defeats the sharing.  Same caution
    docs/elevation.md records for `mul` operand order, on a new construct.

(d) CLAMP AND BRANCH ORDER IS SOURCE ORDER; ARITHMETIC ORDER IS NOT.  sched2
    runs and freely reorders straight-line arithmetic inside a block but cannot
    move anything across a conditional jump.  In ScreenShatter the four
    coordinate computations come out in the REVERSE of the order their clamps
    test them.  Read structure off the branches.

(e) `base + 0x7828` IS NOT A NAMED LOCAL -- the bank rule held again, and
    Tiamat reads 0x7828 too (`ldr r5, =0x7828 / add r5, r10 / ldr r3, [r5]`).
    Anim_Ragnarok's `slot` remains the documented EXCEPTION.

(f) THE HBlank REGISTER-QUEUE PUSH IS ALREADY SOLVED IN THIS BANK.  Take
    `QueuePush1` from src/non_matching/rom_c9000/80cd86c.c verbatim, including
    `SET_IO(REG_IME, REG_ADDR_IME)`, which is what explains the otherwise
    baffling `strh r0, [r0]`.  Do not re-derive it.

(g) FLAGS, RUN ON SCREENSHATTER AND NOT TRANSFERABLE WITHOUT RE-RUNNING:
    `-fno-gcse` and `-fno-cse-follow-jumps` are BOTH EXACTLY INERT there, so
    gcse is ruled out and nothing may be attributed to it.
    `-fno-rerun-cse-after-loop` is NEGATIVE (frame 0x3c -> 0x44).  Flags are
    per-function; re-run before citing.

(h) COUNT A CONSTRUCT ON BOTH SIDES BEFORE NAMING A PASS.  I wrote down that
    cse1 had hoisted a `gBuffer` address function-wide, then counted the pool
    loads: ours 16, reference 16.  One spilled copy of an address is not
    evidence of commoning.
