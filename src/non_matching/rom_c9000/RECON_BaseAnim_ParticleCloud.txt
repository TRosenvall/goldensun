SIGNATURE: TWO PARAMETERS, `(void *context, int variant)`.

*** THE ONE FACT THAT MAKES THIS THE EASIEST OF THE THREE I DID NOT ATTEMPT:
`variant` LIVES IN r8 FOR THE WHOLE FUNCTION and `base` LIVES IN r9. ***
  `mov r8, r1` is the THIRD instruction of the body and `mov r9, r1` (after
  `ldmia`) the sixth.  Neither is ever spilled.  Compare: RapidSlash SPILLS base
  to sp+0x50, Nova spills variant to sp+0x38, Attack keeps base in r11.  Four
  functions, four different answers -- which is the strongest single piece of
  evidence in this batch that A REGISTER EXPECTATION IS NOT A FAMILY LEVER.

FRAME 0x74.  Two slots are set in the prologue before anything else happens:
  sp+0x44 = ctx (g[1], via the walking pointer) and sp+0x34 = 0x10, a literal
  stored to its slot immediately.  That second one is a NAMED `int` LOCAL
  INITIALISED AT THE TOP, i.e. exactly the "name the argument at the top"
  idiom -- declare it early and let it take the slot; do not inline the 0x10.

THE PROLOGUE IS THE FAMILY'S:
  `ldr r3,=iwram_3001eec / ldmia r3!,{r1} / ldr r3,[r3]` -- note it uses NO
  separate `mov r3,r2` copy here because `g` itself is never re-read (there is
  no g[2] and no `- 0x6c` cam load in the prologue), so the walking pointer IS
  the symbol register.  Spell it `pp = iwram_3001eec; base = *pp++; ctx = *pp;`
  with NO separate `g` -- that is the DIFFERENCE from Attack, RapidSlash and
  Nova, all three of which keep `g` alive for a later indexed read and therefore
  need the extra copy.  Getting this wrong costs a `mov` and a pool word.

*** THE VARIANT DISPATCH IS A PLAIN if-CHAIN, NOT A switch, AND NOT THE HYBRID
BaseAnim_Attack NEEDS. ***  Verified off the asm: eight successive
`mov rlo, r8 / cmp rlo, #N / bne` pairs for N = 0,1,2,3,4,5,7 selecting
_FILE_a0 / _FILE_bb / _FILE_a3 / _FILE_c0 / _FILE_bb / _FILE_b7 / (0x18 ...).
There is NO `.word` jump table and NO duplicated `cmp #N` -- so neither a switch
(which would table over a contiguous 0..7) nor emit_case_nodes.  This is the
THIRD dispatch shape in four functions: Breath is a real switch with a five-entry
table, Attack is if/if/switch, this is a pure chain.  READ IT OFF THE ASM EVERY
TIME; the brief's rule is right and the family gives no default.
  The repeated `mov rlo, r8` before each `cmp` is the batch-306 tell for a
  subject in a callee-saved HIGH register -- but here the ROM ITSELF does it, so
  it is the TARGET, not the defect batch 306 fixed.  Do NOT brace-scope your way
  out of it; `variant` is a parameter with a whole-function live range and the
  ROM pays the `mov` eight times on purpose.
