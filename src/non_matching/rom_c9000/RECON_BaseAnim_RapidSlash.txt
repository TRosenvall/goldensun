FRAME 0x8c, and it is the ONE PLACE THIS FUNCTION BREAKS THE FAMILY PATTERN:
  *** `base` IS SPILLED TO sp+0x50 AND RELOADED, over and over
  (`ldr r0,[sp,#0x50]`, `ldr r4,[sp,#0x50]`, `ldr r2,[sp,#0x50]` ...). ***

  every other function in this family trains.

  0x3c (variant<<3), 0x38 (&vec at sp+0x74), 0x34, 0x30 (base+0x7828),

*** THE STRUCTURAL KEY: .Leed3e IS A 7-BYTE-PER-VARIANT PARAMETER RECORD. ***
Every bound, count and sub-selector in this function is a byte out of one table
indexed by `variant * 7 + k`.  The ROM builds the index as
`lsl r5,variant,#3 / sub r2,r5,variant` (that is variant*8 - variant) and then
adds a small constant, and `variant<<3` is HOISTED TO sp+0x3c and reused at every
site.  Verified k values in use: +0 (picks _FILE_b5 vs _FILE_b6), +1 (a 4-way
palette selector -> _FILE_8d / _FILE_a0 / _FILE_b6 / _FILE_b4), +2 (the outer
repeat count, spilled to sp+0x34), +3 (sp+0x20), +4 (the per-repeat stride,
multiplied by the outer counter), +5 (the gate on the whole animation body --
`if (tbl[variant*7+5] == 0)` skips to the epilogue), +6 (sp+0x24).
  WRITE `int v7 = variant * 8 - variant;` AS ITS OWN STATEMENT and index
  `Leed3e[v7 + k]` off it.  Two reasons, both already documented in this tree:
  the derived value has MANY uses (batch 306's more-than-one-use precondition is
  satisfied many times over), and the ROM demonstrably hoists `variant<<3` to a
  stack slot rather than folding it, which is the batch-298 "distribute the shift
  by hand" tell.  Do NOT write `variant * 7`; gcc's synth_mult will pick its own
  shape and the ROM's is `<<3` then subtract.
  The +1 selector's dispatch is emit_case_nodes over THREE case nodes
  (`cmp r3,#1 / beq / cmp r3,#1 / bgt / cmp r3,#0 / beq / b` then `cmp r3,#2`),
  i.e. a `switch` with cases 0, 1, 2 and a default -- the duplicated `cmp #1` is
  gcc's own, exactly as in BaseAnim_Attack's file dispatch.

TWO THINGS TO EXPECT AND NOT MISREAD:
  1. r12 AND r14 ARE LOOP INVARIANTS THE ROM ALLOCATES, in the tile-copy double
     loop at .Le2a12: `mov r12, 0xa2<<7` and `mov r14, 0x90<<1` set once outside,
     compared against inside (`cmp r5,r14`).  Per elevation.md's Func_80c11ec
     note, r12 and lr ARE allocatable in Thumb and the ROM does use them -- so
     KEEP "lr" AND "r12" OUT OF ANY CLOBBER LIST, and do not read their presence
     as inline asm.  Both are ordinary named locals assigned before the loop.
  2. The copy loop's destination index is `(i/2) + row + (0xa2<<7)` with the
     source a separate incrementing byte pointer, and `strb r2,[r4,r3]` is
     register+register on a RELOADED base -- so the base is the spilled `base`,
     reloaded inside the loop (`ldr r4,[sp,#0x50]`).  Another place the spill is
     the thing to reproduce.
